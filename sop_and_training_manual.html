3 Daily Follow-Up Execution Workflow (SOP)

1
Step 1: Check Morning Attention Banner & Schedule

Open the dashboard. Review the top Control Alert Banner. Navigate to Follow-up Schedule and select your name from the Followper dropdown. Click the Due today or Overdue tab.

2
Step 2: Review Customer Balances & Bill Breakdown

Click Bills to view individual invoice dates, policy due dates, and aging breakdown. Click History to review previous conversation notes before calling.

3
Step 3: Call Customer & Log Actual Interaction

Click Update. Verify Actual follow-up date (defaults to today). Enter Contact Person, choose Contact Mode (Phone call / WhatsApp / Email / In person), and select the appropriate Action Status.

Action Status Reference Guide:

Action Status When to Use System Behavior & Rules
Promise to Pay (PTP) Party commits to pay on an exact future date. Mandatory Fields: Expected Amount (₹) & Promise Date.
Next Follow-up date is automatically locked to the PTP date.
Payment Delayed Party requests a few days grace period. Enter conversation remark and set a new follow-up date (default +2 days).
Not Responding Phone unanswered, switched off, or party unavailable. Schedules next follow-up call within 24–48 hours.
Dispute Commercial dispute regarding rates, discount or terms. Requires descriptive remark; flagged for management review.
Claim Matter Quality dispute, damaged goods, or material return. Mandatory: Claim / Complaint Number. Creates ticket in CRM Escalations.
WhatsApp Complaint Issue raised by customer via WhatsApp support. Mandatory: WhatsApp Complaint Number / Reason. Creates ticket in CRM Escalations.
Escalated Account uncooperative or requires senior intervention. Mandatory: Select target (Saurav Bhai, Sales HOD, Accounts, CRM, or Masters). Account ownership transfers immediately to the target manager.

4 Daily Rokad & FIFO Payment Settlement (SOP)

All received payments must be entered through Daily Rokad. The system strictly applies the FIFO (First-In, First-Out) Principle to settle the oldest outstanding invoices first.

1
Step 1: Open Daily Rokad Entry

Navigate to Daily Rokad in the sidebar > click + Record payment.

2
Step 2: Enter Transaction Details

Fill in: Received Date, Payment Mode (Bank transfer / Cheque / UPI / Cash), Receipt / UTR Number, and Total Received Amount (₹).

3
Step 3: Select Marka & Review FIFO Allocation

Select the paying Marka from the dropdown. The system automatically calculates invoice settlement:

  • SETTLED ✓: Oldest bill fully paid and balance becomes ₹0.
  • PART ◐: Bill partially cleared with remaining balance carried forward.
4
Step 4: Post Payment to Cloud Database

Click Post to Rokad. The system updates bill balances, records the transaction in Daily Rokad, adds a payment timeline entry to the Marka's ledger, and reschedules follow-up for remaining balances.

5 GP Lock Policy & Credit Control

GP Lock Trigger Rule: Any Marka whose scheduled follow-up remains overdue by 19 days or more is automatically placed in GP Lock (Hold).

Operational Restrictions under GP Lock:

  1. Immediate Billing & Gate Pass Freeze: No new dispatches, billings, or goods release are permitted under any circumstances.
  2. Schedule Isolation: Account is removed from the active daily schedule and moved to the dedicated GP Lock List (#gplock).
  3. Management Authorization: Release from GP Lock requires direct approval from Saurav Bhai or Accounts HOD upon payment receipt or formal written guarantee.

6 CRM Claims & Dispute Resolution (SOP)

When a Followper logs a Claim Matter or WhatsApp Complaint, a ticket is created in the CRM Escalations module.

1
Step 1: Open CRM Escalations Log

Navigate to CRM Escalations in sidebar > find the open claim > click Resolve & Settle.

2
Step 2: Select Resolution Mode

Choose: Discount / Debit Note, Goods Return / Credit Note, Mutual Settlement, or Rejected.

3
Step 3: Select Affected Invoices & Settle

Check the box for the specific bill(s) against which the claim amount is deducted. Enter the discount amount > click Confirm & Resolve Ticket. The bill balance is deducted immediately, and an audit trail is logged.
